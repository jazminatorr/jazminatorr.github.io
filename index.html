About me

A little about myself

👤

I'm a software engineer specializing in test automation for safety-critical and high-tech systems. Over 7+ years I've built and led automated testing pipelines for autonomous vehicles at Cruise and Zoox, a robotics-based HIL platform for VR tracking at Oculus, and mission-critical military flight training software at Leidos.

I thrive where the stakes are high and the hardware is unforgiving — designing test architectures that catch failures before they ever reach the real world. I bring equal parts engineering rigor and curiosity to every system I work on, whether it's a suspension ECU, a camera perception stack, or a SLAM tracking algorithm.

Outside of work, I recharge in the outdoors — mountain biking, snowboarding, and free diving keep me grounded — and on the dance floor with salsa.
